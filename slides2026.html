Background

With the release of version 9 of breast protocol, we concluded that it is the right time to develop a tool can be used and referenced by physicians and EHR vendors.

The goal is to simplify a complex staging process by automating the interpretation of AJCC rules, reducing manual effort, and ensuring consistency across different treatment pathways.

Why It Is Needed

  • Version 9 breast staging incorporates several disease-specific factors
  • Different users or systems may interpret complex staging rules differently

A centralized rules engine helps:

  • Improve consistency
  • Reduce manual interpretation
  • Simplify implementation
  • Support future updates to staging rules
  • Provide a common reference for testing and validation

What Makes AJCC’s Calculator Unique

EHRs and vendors may have produced their calculators in the past but:

  • The biggest differentiator is that AJCC itself owns the source rules.
  • The logic is reviewed with the subject-matter experts who develop the standard.
  • That gives us one authoritative calculation rather than different vendor interpretations.

How Does the Application Work?

The calculator follows a structured decision process:

  1. User selects the disease site and staging context.
  2. The calculator identifies the applicable staging variables.
  3. Valid values are presented for each required data item.
  4. The user provides the relevant clinical or pathological values.
  5. The rules engine evaluates the combination against AJCC staging definitions.
  6. The corresponding stage or response category is returned.
