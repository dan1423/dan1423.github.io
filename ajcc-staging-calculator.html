This version of the AJCC Breast Staging Calculator is a prototype and uses simplified and limited test rules. Some TNM combinations and post-therapy responses may produce incomplete, “Unknown” or incorrect results. This version is not to be used for patient care, treatment decisions, documentation, or registry reporting. Physicians are encouraged to share feedback on unclear wording and features that would make the calculator more useful in comparison to calculators already in use.

Logic: TNM and biomarkers (grade, ER, PR, HER2) are required before any stage is calculated · Clinical → cTNM stage · Pathological → pTNM stage · De Novo (cM1) → metastatic sites + T, grade, histology, ER, PR, HER2 → Stage IV · Post-therapy → cTNM (cM0) + biomarkers (entered once) + ypTNM → Response (cTNM vs ypTNM) → Post-therapy stage
