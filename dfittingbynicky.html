How a fitting works

Walk in or call ahead. First visit usually takes twenty minutes.

FIRST

Come in with the garment

Or with a photo of what you want made. Wear the shoes you'll pair it with — hem length depends on them.

THEN

Get measured and pinned

You'll try it on, we'll pin it, and you'll see the shape before anything is cut. Price is quoted on the spot.

AFTER

Pick up, try again

Come back and try it on before you leave. If something's off, it goes back on the machine that day.
